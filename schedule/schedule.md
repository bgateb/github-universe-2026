Agent-driven deployment beyond localhost, shared securely with your team (Sponsored)
You and your agent just built something that works on your laptop. Now you want your
team to use it, without exposing it to the whole internet or wrestling with passwords and
credentials to lock it down. In this demo, you simply ask your agent to publish it, and it
handles the rest: opening a pull request, deploying the app, and putting it behind private,
identity-based access that only you can reach. Sharing it with your team is just as easy,
and every change to who can get in is captured in a diff you approve. The result is a way
to ship and share work that's private by default, where identity decides who gets access
and the review trail records why. You'll leave knowing how agents can take your work
from localhost to your team securely, without public URLs, shared passwords, or
credentials to manage.

17 agents, one SDLC: How Levi Strauss reimagined development on GitHub
Levi Strauss & Co. built an enterprise multi-agent software development lifecycle on
GitHub Copilot's custom agents platform. Seventeen specialized Al agents now
handle requirements, spec generation, automated testing, security scanning,
accessibility validation, and deployment, all connected to Jira, Confluence, and New
Relic through MCP. Every pull request is reviewed by 5 to 11 independent agents that
automatically fix issues and commit back to the branch, and every workflow has
human approval gates built in. The result is a development lifecycle where agents do
the heavy, repetitive work and people stay in control of the decisions that matter. This
session shares the real story: the architecture behind the system, the lessons learned
building it (including what didn't work at first), and a practical view of what a multi-
agent SDLC means for enterprise teams. You'll leave understanding how the pieces fit
together, where human oversight belongs, and how to think about adopting agents
across your own lifecycle rather than in a single step.

Speakers
Jenny Patchen / Sr. Director / Shared Platforms & Services / Levi Strauss & Co.
